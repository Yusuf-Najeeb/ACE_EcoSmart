EcoSmart Screen Specification

The eight screens below cover the generator and recycler flows. Role-specific content appears after sign-in; related steps are combined to keep the pilot simple.

1. Account entry and email verification

Purpose: Let a generator or recycler create an account and prove control of their email address.

Elements

  1. EcoSmart logo and page title.
  2. Role selector: I have recyclable waste or I buy recyclable waste.
  3. Full-name, email-address, and area/neighbourhood inputs.
  4. Continue button.
  5. email-code input and Verify email button, shown after Continue.

Interactions

  • Role selection stores generator or recycler.
  • Continue validates inputs and sends an email code.
  • Verify email sends generators to Screen 3 and recyclers to Screen 2. Invalid/expired codes show an error and resend option.

2. Recycler verification application

Purpose: Let a recycler submit the evidence required for administrator approval.

Elements

  1. Header with account name and verification status.
  2. Recycler/business name and contact details.
  3. Government-ID type, number, and document upload.
  4. Recycler photo upload.
  5. Licence/registration type, number, and document upload.
  6. Area/neighbourhood input.
  7. Submit for approval button.
  8. Pending, approved, or rejected status panel with administrator note.

Interactions

  • Uploads attach evidence to the application.
  • Submission saves a pending application and prevents listings/offers until approval.
  • After approval, Continue to marketplace setup opens Screen 4. A rejection lets the recycler correct and resubmit.

3. Check my waste

Purpose: Let a generator identify supported waste and see recyclability guidance before finding a recycler.

Elements

  1. Header with area/neighbourhood and account menu.
  2. Scan item and Upload photo buttons.
  3. Manual material selector: cardboard, PET plastic bottles, aluminium, brass, or glass.
  4. Selected-image preview or selected-material summary.
  5. Recyclability result and recycling tip.
  6. Find recyclers button.
  7. Not supported in this pilot message and Choose another material button, when applicable.

Interactions

  • Scanning/uploading sends an image for intake; uncertain results require manual selection.
  • Selecting a material shows its guidance and tip.
  • Find recyclers opens Screen 5; Choose another material clears an unsupported result.

4. Recycler marketplace dashboard

Purpose: Let an approved recycler configure materials, estimates, availability, and view matching requests.

Elements

  1. Header with recycler name and verification badge.
  2. Availability switch.
  3. Accepted-material list.
  4. Price input, unit selector, and estimate-not-final label for each accepted material.
  5. Save marketplace settings button.
  6. Incoming-request list and View request button.
  7. Transaction-records link.

Interactions

  • Availability includes/excludes the recycler from matching.
  • Saving publishes non-binding estimates.
  • View request opens Screen 6; records opens Screen 8.

5. Recycler matches and listing

Purpose: Let a generator compare matching recyclers and send one selected recycler a listing.

Elements

  1. Header, back button, and selected material.
  2. Guidance summary and estimate-not-final reminder.
  3. Recycler cards: name, area, material, estimate, unit, availability, and Select.
  4. Listing form: description, photo, quantity, location, pickup/drop-off preference.
  5. Send listing button.
  6. No-match empty state.

Interactions

  • Select chooses one recycler only.
  • Send listing creates a request for that recycler and opens Screen 6 in generator view.
  • A decline returns here with other matches; the listing is never broadcast automatically.

6. Listing and handover

Purpose: Let matched parties review a listing, respond, share contacts, and agree pickup/drop-off.

Elements

  1. Header with status and transaction ID.
  2. Material, photo, quantity, location, and preference summary.
  3. Estimated price and unit labelled as an estimate.
  4. Recycler-only Accept, Decline, decline reason, and arrangement controls.
  5. Shared contact panel after acceptance.
  6. Optional chat thread, message input, and optional Start call.
  7. Recycler-only Handover completed / continue to inspection.

Interactions

  • Accept saves the arrangement, reveals contacts, and enables communication.
  • Decline returns the generator to Screen 5.
  • Chat/call communicate only with the matched party. Continuing opens Screen 7 after direct handover outside the app.

7. Inspection, final offer, and decision

Purpose: Let the recycler fund a final offer after inspection and let the generator accept or reject it within 24 hours.

Elements

  1. Header with status, transaction ID, and expiry countdown.
  2. Recycler-only actual weight/quantity, unit, and inspection-note fields.
  3. Recycler-only final-amount input and funding panel.
  4. Recycler-only Fund and send final offer.
  5. Generator final-offer summary after funding.
  6. Generator Accept final offer, Reject final offer, and rejection-reason input.
  7. Completed, rejected, expired, or payment-failed result panel.

Interactions

  • Funding validates inspection, funds the exact amount, then sends the offer; unfunded offers cannot be sent.
  • Acceptance releases the full amount with zero pilot commission and sends both parties to Screen 8.
  • Rejection or expiry makes no payment and sends both parties to Screen 8.

8. Records and administration

Purpose: Let users view time-stamped records and let administrators manage recyclers, materials, and marketplace records.

Elements

  1. Role-specific header and account menu.
  2. User transaction-record list with status/date filters.
  3. Record timeline: intake, estimate, listing, response, arrangement, inspection, final offer, decision, and payment.
  4. Payment reference and payout status.
  5. Administrator recycler-approval queue with Approve/Reject.
  6. Administrator supported-material catalogue controls.
  7. Administrator marketplace-record search and detail panel.

Interactions

  • Selecting a record shows its history.
  • Approval enables Screen 4; rejection updates verification and stores a note.
  • Catalogue changes update the materials in Screens 3 and 4. This screen does not provide disputes, transport, ratings, or commission handling.

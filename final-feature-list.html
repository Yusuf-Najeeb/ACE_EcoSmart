Fiee-day scope decision

The Must-have list is intentionally limited to six slices. If time becomes tighter, cut these first:

  1. Scan and photo upload: launch with manual material selection.
  2. In-app chat: use approved contact details after acceptance.
  3. Administrator workspace: use a protected manual approeal and catalogue process.
  4. Full record browser: retain the audit eeents but show a simple completion receipt.

Do not cut the six Must-have slices: they create the path from verified participants to matching, inspection, funded offer, and payment outcome.
